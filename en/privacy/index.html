Privacy notice

This page explains how the data of those who write through the form on travel.mattiaferro.com is handled.

Data controller

Mattia Ferro. Contact: mattia.ferro@fora.travel.

What data, and why

The form collects your name, your email and, if you wish to share it, the destination you are thinking about. They serve one purpose only: getting back to you for the conversation you asked for. No newsletter, no mailing lists, no sharing with third parties for marketing.

How the data travels

The form is handled by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which delivers the message to the controller's email inbox and acts as the technical processor of the submission. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may keep technical access logs.

Cookies

This site uses no cookies and no tracking or analytics tools.

Retention

Messages are kept for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any relationship that follows.

Bookings

Bookings are contracted and executed by Fora Travel Inc., New York, which processes booking data under its own policies.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification or deletion of your data at any time by writing to mattia.ferro@fora.travel.
